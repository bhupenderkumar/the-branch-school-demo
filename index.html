Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum that challenges, inspires, and prepares every student.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

Our Pre-K program is designed for children aged 3-4, providing a fun and engaging introduction to the world of learning. Our experienced teachers use play-based activities to foster social, emotional, and cognitive development.

📚

Elementary

Our Elementary program serves students in grades K-4, focusing on building a strong foundation in reading, writing, math, and science. Our small class sizes and personalized approach ensure that each child receives the attention they need to succeed.

📊

Middle/Upper

Our Middle and Upper School programs serve students in grades 5-8, providing a challenging and supportive environment that prepares students for high school and beyond. Our curriculum is designed to foster critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving skills.
