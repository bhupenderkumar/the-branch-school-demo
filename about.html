Our Mission & History

The Branch School was founded in 2005 with the mission of providing a unique, personalized education to students in Houston's vibrant inner loop. Our small student body allows us to tailor our approach to each child's needs and interests, creating a nurturing environment that fosters a love of learning.

Our teaching approach is centered around project-based learning, which encourages students to explore real-world problems and develop innovative solutions. This approach helps our students develop critical thinking skills, creativity, and collaboration – essential for success in the 21st century.

At The Branch School, we believe that education goes beyond the classroom. Our extracurricular programs and activities, such as sports, music, and art, help our students develop important life skills like teamwork, perseverance, and leadership.
