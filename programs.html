Our Programs

STEM Program

Our STEM program provides students with hands-on experience in science, technology, engineering, and math, preparing them for success in an increasingly complex world.

Fine Arts Program

The Branch School's fine arts program offers students the opportunity to explore their creativity through music, art, and drama, helping them develop self-expression and confidence.

Athletics Program

Our athletics program promotes physical fitness, teamwork, and sportsmanship, with a range of sports and activities available to students of all ages and abilities.

Language Arts Program

The Branch School's language arts program focuses on developing strong reading, writing, and communication skills, with a emphasis on literature, grammar, and vocabulary.

Character Education Program

Our character education program teaches students important values such as respect, empathy, and responsibility, helping them become caring and compassionate members of their community.

Summer Camp Program

The Branch School's summer camp program offers a fun and engaging way for students to stay active, learn new skills, and make friends during the summer months.
