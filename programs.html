Our Programs

Early Childhood Program

Our early childhood program is designed for students aged 3-5 and focuses on developing social, emotional, and cognitive skills through play-based learning.

Elementary School Program

Our elementary school program provides a comprehensive education in core subjects, including language arts, mathematics, science, and social studies, as well as specialized classes in art, music, and physical education.

Middle School Program

Our middle school program is designed to support students during a critical phase of development, with a focus on academic rigor, character development, and extracurricular activities.

Summer Enrichment Program

Our summer enrichment program offers a range of activities and courses that allow students to explore new interests, develop new skills, and stay engaged during the summer months.

After-School Program

Our after-school program provides a safe and supportive environment for students to complete homework, participate in extracurricular activities, and socialize with peers.

Specialized Support Services

We offer specialized support services, including academic tutoring, counseling, and speech therapy, to ensure that each student receives the support they need to succeed.
