Academics & Enrichment

Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum designed to challenge, inspire, and prepare every student for success.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

Our Pre-K program is designed for children aged 3-4, providing a fun and engaging introduction to the world of learning. Our experienced teachers use play-based activities to foster social, emotional, and cognitive development.

📚

Elementary

Our Elementary program serves students in grades K-4, focusing on building a strong foundation in reading, writing, math, and science. Our small class sizes and personalized approach ensure that each child receives the attention they need to succeed.

📊

Middle/Upper

Our Middle and Upper School programs serve students in grades 5-8, providing a challenging and supportive environment that prepares students for high school and beyond. Our curriculum is designed to foster critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving skills.

⚗️

STEM

Our STEM program is designed to inspire students to explore the wonders of science, technology, engineering, and math. Through hands-on activities and projects, our students develop essential skills in critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving.

🎨

Arts

Our Arts program offers students the opportunity to explore their creative side through music, art, drama, and dance. Our experienced teachers provide guidance and support as students develop their skills and confidence.

🏟️

Athletics

At The Branch School, we believe that sports and physical activity are essential for a healthy and happy lifestyle. Our athletics program offers students the opportunity to develop teamwork skills, build confidence, and stay active through a variety of sports and activities.
